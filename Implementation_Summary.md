## 🎓 Student Workflow Summary

### Student Daily Process (2 minutes):
1. **Open Cockpit Tools** (or find in system tray)
2. **Find their account** in the dashboard
3. **Click "Switch"** to activate their account
4. **Launch their preferred IDE** (credentials auto-injected)
5. **Work normally** (quota monitoring automatic)
6. **When done** - Switch back to account selection

### Benefits for Students:
- ✅ **Easy account switching** - No constant logging in/out

- ✅ **Quick setup** - 5-minute initial setup, 2-minute daily use
