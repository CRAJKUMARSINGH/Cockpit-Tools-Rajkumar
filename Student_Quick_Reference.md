## Quick Setup (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Open Cockpit Tools
- Look for Cockpit Tools icon in system tray (bottom right)
- Double-click to open, or find it in Start Menu

### Step 2: Add Your Account
1. Click on your preferred IDE (Cursor, Windsurf, etc.)
2. Click "Add Account"
3. Choose "OAuth Sign-in"
4. Enter your email and password
5. Complete authentication

### Step 3: Test It
1. Find your account in the dashboard
2. Click "Switch"
3. Click "Launch"
4. Verify you're logged in

## Daily Use (2 Minutes)

### Start Your Session:
1. Open Cockpit Tools
2. Find your account
3. Click "Switch"
4. Launch your IDE

### End Your Session:
1. Close your IDE
2. Return to Cockpit Tools
3. Click "Switch" to deselect
4. (Optional) Close Cockpit Tools

## Need Help?

**Common Issues:**
- "Can't find account" → You need to create the AI IDE account first
- "Wrong password" → Reset your password on the platform website
- "Not switching" → Restart Cockpit Tools and try again

**Ask Your Instructor For Help With:**
- Account setup issues
- Authentication problems
- Quota questions
- Technical difficulties
